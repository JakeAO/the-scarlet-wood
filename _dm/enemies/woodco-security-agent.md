# WoodCo Security Agent

**Common Humanoid** (DR: 20)

*Harvest: 1d6 Material*

## Description
Dressed in grey-and-green tactical gear bearing the stylized pine tree logo of WoodCo, these agents are professional, disciplined, and heavily armed. They are not merely security guards; they are a private military force authorized to protect corporate assets with lethal force.

## Abilities & Traits
*   **Target Priority (Martialist):** When a melee attack reduces a target to 0 DHP, retarget remaining damage to another creature in range.
*   **Grappler:** Gains an additional point of favor on tests made to grapple.
*   **Tactical Movement:** Spend 3 stamina to add Stunt (1) to Travel distance.
*   **Parry:** When Blocking a melee attack, spend 1 interrupt action + 5 stamina to roll melee damage instead of Block Rating.

## Actions
*   **Tactical Machete (3 Actions):** 2m range. 8d8 Physical damage.
*   **Assault Rifle (3 Actions):** 20/40m range. 4d10 + 7 Physical damage.
