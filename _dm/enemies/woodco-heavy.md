# WoodCo Heavy

**Common Humanoid** (DR: 39)

*Harvest: 1d8 Material, 1d4 Refinement*

## Description
Hulking figures encased in experimental composite armor, WoodCo Heavies serve as the anvil to the Agents' hammer. They carry heavy magitech weaponry—often a whining force-blade chainsaw or a heavy energy repeater—and can shrug off blows that would fell a tree.

## Abilities & Traits
*   **Cleave (Militant):** When a melee attack reduces a target to 0 DHP, retarget remaining damage to another creature in range.
*   **Overwatch (Militant):** Whenever another creature takes the Attack action, spend 8 stamina to take the Attack action using interrupt actions.
*   **Armored Advance:** Spend 3 stamina to add Stunt (1) to Travel distance.

## Actions
*   **Magitech Chainsaw (1 Action):** 1m range. 4d4 Energy damage. (Force Blade profile)
*   **Heavy Repeater (2 Actions):** 20/40m range. 4d8 Energy damage.
