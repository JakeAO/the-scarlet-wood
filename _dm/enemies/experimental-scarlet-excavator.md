# Experimental Scarlet Excavator

**Common Construct** (DR: 37)

*Harvest: 1d10 Material, 1d4 Power Components*

## Description
A horrifying fusion of industrial technology and stolen magic. This bulldozer-like machine has no driver; its cab is filled with pulsing scarlet wood circuitry. The heavy blade is stained with earth and sap, and it moves with a lurching, predatory intent.

## Abilities & Traits
*   **Construct Nature:** Immune to Breathe/Eat/Sleep/Fatigue/Unconscious.
*   **Remote Control:** If controlled by a caster, ignores Psyche damage (damage goes to caster).
*   **Magic Resistance:** +1 Favor to resist spells.
*   **Trample (Brute):** When a melee attack reduces a target to 0 DHP, retarget remaining damage to another creature in range.

## Actions
*   **Crushing Blade (1 Action):** 0m range. 4d4 Physical damage.
*   **Hydraulic Slam (2 Actions):** 0m range. 1d8 + 9 Physical damage.
    *   *Effect:* Spend up to 3 additional turn actions to deal +2d8 Physical damage per action.
