# WoodCo Logger

**Common Humanoid** (DR: 13)

*Harvest: 1d4 Material*

## Description
WoodCo Loggers are the backbone of the corporation's timber operations—hardened workers clad in high-visibility vests and heavy canvas gear. They wield industrial timber axes and chainsaws not as weapons of war, but as tools of their trade. While not trained combatants like Security Agents, these workers are strong, accustomed to physical labor, and capable of defending themselves when threatened. They prefer to avoid conflict, but a cornered logger with a running chainsaw is not to be underestimated.

## Abilities & Traits
*   **Woodworker's Grip:** Gains an additional point of favor on tests made to grapple.
*   **Worker's Instinct:** When reduced below 8 DHP, may spend 3 stamina to take the Travel action using interrupt actions as if they were turn actions.
*   **Aggressive Mobility (Aggressive):** When this creature takes the Travel action, it may spend 6 stamina to increase its Land/Water/Air travel by 2 (Stunt × 2 stacks) for the rest of the action.
*   **Power Strike (Aggressive):** When this creature makes an Attack, it may spend 4 additional turn actions and 24 stamina to attack a target at random and deal ×3 damage.

## Actions
*   **Timber Axe (2 Turn Actions):** 0m range. 1d8 + 4 Physical damage.
    *   *Effect:* This creature may spend up to 3 additional turn actions, dealing an additional 2d8 Physical damage per additional turn action.
*   **Wide Swing (4 Turn Actions, 8 Stamina):** 0m range. 3d8 Physical damage to all creatures within melee range.
*   **Chainsaw (3 Turn Actions):** 0m range. 4d4 Physical damage.
