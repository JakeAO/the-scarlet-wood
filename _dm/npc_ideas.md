# The Scarlet Wood – NPC Ideas

This document summarizes notable NPCs introduced or implied in *The Scarlet Wood* campaign, organized by faction. Each entry includes their role in the story, how they may interact with the party, and how they function in the background narrative.

---

## Sky-Sailors

### Orven Taloncrest (Hawk)
**Role:** Former leader of the Sky-Sailors; Captain and trainer of the Junior Rangers.

- Acts as the party’s primary mentor and handler during early sessions.
- Balances discipline with protection of the PCs before the council.
- Coordinates reconnaissance against WoodCo once the threat becomes clear.
- Background role: Oversees long-range scouting, gathers intelligence beyond the Wood, and acts as a stabilizing voice between factions.

---

## Moss-Fur Clan

### Marla Mossfur (Mouse)
**Role:** Council representative; voice of common folk and families.

- Advocates caution and community safety over escalation.
- Serves as a moral counterbalance to more aggressive plans.
- Background role: Gauges civilian sentiment and fallout from PC actions.

### Matron Hilda (Fox)
**Role:** Senior Moss-Fur leader.

- Pragmatic and politically minded.
- Supports resistance but fears provoking overwhelming retaliation.
- Background role: Manages logistics, food security, and refugee planning if conflict worsens.

---

## Deep-Root Kin

### Mistgleam (Spider)
**Role:** Elder scholar and interpreter of manfolk language and lore.

- Assists the party in deciphering WoodCo documents.
- Confirms the concept of "resonant samples" and magical extraction.
- Background role: Maintains hidden intelligence networks and archives beneath the Wood.

### Creeve (Snake)
**Role:** Council representative; covert operations advocate.

- Supports preemptive strikes and information warfare.
- Pushes the party toward sabotage and intimidation.
- Background role: Coordinates spies and assassins beyond council approval.

### Sablecoil (Snake)
**Role:** Deep-cover operative.

- Rarely appears openly.
- May provide the party with secret aid or warnings.
- Background role: Acts independently, sometimes destabilizing council unity.

---

## Earth-Movers

### Foreman Bristleback (Beaver)
**Role:** Builder and defensive engineer.

- Focused on fortifications, terrain shaping, and delaying actions.
- Less interested in politics, more in practical solutions.
- Background role: Oversees earthworks, barricades, and forest reshaping to counter vehicles.

---

## Scrap-Smiths

### Rook Gearclaw (Raccoon)
**Role:** Young council member; inventor and analyst.

- Identifies WoodCo markings, logistics patterns, and contracts.
- Designs anti-vehicle traps and countermeasures.
- Background role: Drives technological escalation on the forest’s side.

---

## Antagonists – Local

### Billy Sr. (Human)
**Role:** Head of Lumberjack Billy & Sons (LB&S).

- Greedy, reckless, and largely unaware of the greater magical context.
- Motivated by money and pride.
- Background role: Disposable proxy whose actions justify WoodCo's interest.

### Billy Jr. (Human)
**Role:** Youngest son; at logging camp during infiltration.

- Intelligent but physically less capable than his father and brother.
- Present at the camp during Session 4 infiltration but unaware of PCs' activities.
- His ideas for modernization are dismissed by his family.

### Eli Billy (Human)
**Role:** Oldest son and heir apparent.

- Embodies traditional logging values and physical prowess.
- Set to inherit the business from Billy Sr.
- Shares his father's dismissive attitude toward modernization.

### Tracking / Hunting Dogs
**Role:** Tools of the loggers.

- Used for pursuit, intimidation, and tracking forestkin.
- Not inherently malicious, but lethal if uncontrolled.

---

## Antagonists – WoodCo

### WoodCo Agent (Human)
**Role:** Observer and liaison.

- Present during later LB&S operations.
- Records data and reports interference.
- Background role: Triggers WoodCo’s formal classification of the Scarlet Wood as hostile.

### Dr. Lyle (Human)
**Role:** Head of WoodCo R&D.

- Fully aware of the Scarlet Wood’s latent magic.
- Drives expansion into magical forestry and weaponization.
- Background role: Long-term campaign antagonist and ideological foil.

---

## Narrative Functions to Track
- Mentors vs. Radicals
- Scholars vs. Builders
- Proxies vs. True Antagonists

NPCs should evolve based on the party’s choices, escalating from advisors and obstacles into allies, liabilities, or casualties.
