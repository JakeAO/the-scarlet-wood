# The Scarlet Wood – Future Plot Path

This document summarizes the expected trajectory of the campaign from **Session 7 through Session 20**, organized into **Acts**. It focuses on likely events, faction responses, escalation, and decision points, rather than fixed outcomes. Player choices may alter or bypass individual elements.

---

## Act I – Blood on the Trail (Sessions 7–8)
**Core Theme:** Escalation, consequences, first irreversible violence.

### Session 6: Ripples and Response
- News of the confrontation spreads through the Scarlet Wood.
- Council debates next steps:
  - Fortify defenses against expected WoodCo retaliation
  - Prepare for WoodCo to classify the Wood as hostile
  - Decide whether to go on the offensive or consolidate
- The party may undertake missions to:
  - Gather intelligence on WoodCo's response
  - Spread disinformation about the incident
  - Secure allies within the council

### Session 7: Retaliation Probes
- WoodCo deploys limited countermeasures:
  - Armed survey teams to assess the situation
  - Increased patrols on access roads
  - Attempts to identify the "agitators" from the video footage
- The party undertakes missions to disrupt or mislead these efforts.

---

## Act II – The Living Wood Pushes Back (Sessions 8–11)
**Core Theme:** Resistance, unity, and strategic defense.

### Session 8: Fortifying the Scarlet Wood
- Scrap-Smiths deploy traps, barriers, and improvised defenses.
- Earth-Movers reshape terrain to block heavy vehicles.
- The party helps test, deploy, or defend these measures.

### Session 9: Internal Strain
- Disagreements arise within the council:
  - How far is too far?
  - Is killing manfolk justified?
- One faction may act independently, causing complications.

### Session 10: The Stolen Grove
- WoodCo successfully extracts a **major resonant sample** from a hidden site.
- The party tracks the operation too late to stop it—but learns where it is headed.

### Session 11: Choosing the Battlefield
- The party must decide:
  - Defend the Wood exclusively
  - Or strike beyond it for the first time
- This choice defines Act III’s direction.

---

## Act III – Beyond the Scarlet Wood (Sessions 12–15)
**Core Theme:** Discovery, moral ambiguity, and the true scale of the threat.

### Session 12: Crossing the Boundary
- The party leaves the Wood for the first time.
- They encounter towns, mills, rail depots, or river ports tied to WoodCo.

### Session 13: WoodCo Exposed
- Investigation reveals:
  - The R&D division’s understanding of natural magic
  - Experiments using resonant wood
  - Long-term plans that extend beyond logging

### Session 14: Allies or Enemies
- The party encounters:
  - Sympathetic manfolk workers
  - Exploited laborers
  - Rival interests competing with WoodCo
- Choices here shape the final conflict.

### Session 15: The Price of Sabotage
- A major strike against WoodCo infrastructure succeeds—but causes collateral damage.
- Consequences ripple outward politically and ecologically.

---

## Act IV – The Scarlet Reckoning (Sessions 16–19)
**Core Theme:** Resolution, sacrifice, and legacy.

### Session 16: WoodCo Strikes Back
- WoodCo commits fully:
  - Private security
  - Experimental tools powered by resonant material
  - Coordinated, overwhelming force

### Session 17: The Wood Awakens
- Latent magic in the Scarlet Wood reacts violently.
- Ancient guardians, living terrain, or awakened spirits emerge.
- Control or chaos depends on earlier choices.

### Session 18: Final Confrontation
- The campaign’s climax occurs at one of the following:
  - A WoodCo processing facility
  - A corrupted grove
  - The heart of the Scarlet Wood itself
- The party confronts leadership, ideology, and consequence.

### Session 19: What Remains
- Aftermath and epilogue session.
- Determine:
  - The fate of the Scarlet Wood
  - The future of its factions
  - How the party is remembered (myths, warnings, or martyrs)

---

## Campaign Axes to Track
- **Violence vs. Restraint**
- **Isolation vs. Outreach**
- **Preservation vs. Transformation**

These axes should influence NPC reactions, WoodCo strategy, and the final state of the world.
