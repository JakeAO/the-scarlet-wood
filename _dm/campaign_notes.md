# The Scarlet Wood — Act-Level Campaign Summary

---

## ACT I — THE FIRST WOUND

**Core Vibe:**
Innocence eroding into responsibility; discovery, defiance, and irreversible choice.

**Narrative Purpose:**
Establish the Scarlet Wood as a living homeland, the PCs as youthful forestkin on the edge of adulthood, and the central conflict between ecological magic and industrial exploitation.

**Major Developments:**
- PCs serve as junior rangers and scouts along the forest’s borders
- Discovery of the first clear-cut scar within the Scarlet Wood
- Initial violent encounters with hunters and subcontracted loggers
- Revelation that Lumberjack Billy & Sons are subcontractors for WoodCo
- Discovery that WoodCo is harvesting “resonant” wood samples for research
- Political debate within the Scarlet Wood council over escalation
- Deliberate ambush and destruction of Lumberjack Billy & Sons
- Death of a WoodCo agent; digital evidence transmitted beyond the forest
- WoodCo deploys scarlet-powered bulldozers and establishes a forward operating presence

**Act I Truths Established:**
- The Scarlet Wood contains latent, exploitable magic
- WoodCo is aware of this magic and willing to escalate to claim it
- Violence against the manfolk is now documented and undeniable
- The PCs are no longer trainees—they are symbols and catalysts

**End State:**
The Scarlet Wood has crossed from secrecy into open conflict. There is no return to deniability.

---

## ACT II — OPENING THE WAR

**Core Vibe:**
Guerrilla resistance, fear tempered by resolve, asymmetric escalation.

**Narrative Purpose:**
Show what sustained resistance looks like when facing an industrial power. Establish WoodCo as a persistent, adaptive antagonist and force the forestfolk to confront the cost of continued conflict.

**Major Developments:**
- WoodCo expands logging operations and fortified camps near the Wood
- Increased use of surveillance, scouts, and scarlet-powered machinery
- The Scarlet Wood factions organize raids, sabotage, and misinformation
- Scrap-Smith innovations create anti-vehicle traps and countermeasures
- The forest itself begins reacting more aggressively to intrusion

**Key Tensions:**
- Effectiveness vs. moral erosion
- Innovation vs. becoming the enemy
- Protection of civilians vs. proactive strikes

**End State:**
The conflict becomes normalized. Both sides adapt, and the war becomes sustained rather than reactive.

---

## ACT III — THE SCARLET MACHINES

**Core Vibe:**
Unease, revelation, industrial horror, magical backlash.

**Narrative Purpose:**
Reveal the full consequences of WoodCo’s research. Shift the conflict from territorial to existential by showing what scarlet magic becomes when fully weaponized.

**Major Developments:**
- Deployment of limited numbers of experimental scarlet-powered constructs
- Encounters with unstable or semi-sentient magitech
- Evidence that harvested scarlet wood degrades or mutates outside the forest
- The Scarlet Wood exhibits signs of pain, instability, or awakening
- PCs uncover deeper truths about the forest’s magic and ancient safeguards

**Key Tensions:**
- Destroying machines vs. saving what remains of the magic within them
- Short-term survival vs. long-term ecological damage

**End State:**
The true stakes are revealed: misuse of the Scarlet Wood threatens more than its borders—it threatens its existence.

---

## ACT IV — THE WAR FOR THE WOOD

**Core Vibe:**
Desperation, sacrifice, unity under strain, large-scale resistance.

**Narrative Purpose:**
Bring the conflict to its most volatile phase. The Scarlet Wood and WoodCo are fully engaged, and the cost of failure is total loss.

**Major Developments:**
- Coordinated forest-wide resistance efforts
- Large-scale assaults on logging camps and supply routes
- WoodCo escalates with hardened magitech and elite R&D assets
- Internal fractures within the Scarlet Wood council intensify
- The forest begins acting with will and agency beyond any faction’s control

**Key Tensions:**
- Unity vs. fragmentation
- Survival vs. self-destruction
- Command vs. autonomy

**End State:**
Everything is committed. The conflict can no longer be contained or paused.

---

## ACT V — WHAT MUST ENDURE

**Core Vibe:**
Resolve, grief, transformation, hard-earned hope.

**Narrative Purpose:**
Deliver the campaign’s climax and force a reckoning with the consequences of every prior choice.

**Major Developments:**
- Final confrontation with WoodCo R&D leadership and their ultimate magitech
- Direct battle involving Dr. Lyle and a flagship scarlet-powered construct
- The Scarlet Wood fully manifests as an active participant
- PCs make defining choices about mercy, annihilation, or withdrawal

**End State:**
WoodCo’s campaign against the Scarlet Wood is broken—but the forest is irrevocably changed.

---

## EPILOGUE — THE SCARLET LEGACY

**Core Vibe:**
Bittersweet endurance.

**Narrative Purpose:**
Show the long shadow of resistance and survival.

**Possible Outcomes:**
- The Scarlet Wood becomes protected, feared, or mythologized
- The forest’s magic stabilizes—or forever bears scars
- New generations grow up shaped by the PCs’ choices

The final question remains:

> **What was saved—and what was lost to do so?**
