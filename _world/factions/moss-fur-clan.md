## Overview

The Moss-Fur clan represents the common folk of the forest. They are typically small mammals such as squirrels, rabbits, and mice who have adapted to life in the underbrush. Known for their resourcefulness and community-oriented nature, the Moss-Fur clan thrives on cooperation and mutual support.

## Notable Members

- **[Marla Mossfur]({{ "/world/npcs/marla-mossfur/" | relative_url }})** - Mouse council representative
- **[Matron Hilda]({{ "/world/npcs/matron-hilda/" | relative_url }})** - Fox elder and clan leader

## Values

The Moss-Fur Clan values community, cooperation, and the simple joys of forest life. They believe that no individual is too small to make a difference and that mutual support creates strength far beyond what any creature could achieve alone. Good-natured and highly social, they prioritize kindness, sharing resources, and maintaining the social bonds that keep the Scarlet Wood united.

## Role

The Moss-Fur Clan serves as the backbone of daily life in the Scarlet Wood. They are the laborers, farmers, gatherers, and caretakers who ensure the forest community has food, supplies, and care. Their skill in foraging and utilizing the forest's resources efficiently keeps everyone fed and supplied. They represent the heart of the Wood—not the most powerful or specialized, but essential to the survival and wellbeing of all.
