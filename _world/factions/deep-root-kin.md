## Overview

The Deep-Root Kin are the faction comprised primarily of the 'others' of the forest. Snakes, spiders, and other creatures that dwell in the deep woods band together under this banner. They are known for their cunning and adaptability, often using the dense forest to their advantage in both defense and offense.

- **[Mistgleam]({{ "/world/npcs/mistgleam/" | relative_url }})** - Spider translator with expertise in human documents
- **[Creeve]({{ "/world/npcs/creeve/" | relative_url }})** - Snake council representative
- **[Sablecoil]({{ "/world/npcs/sablecoil/" | relative_url }})** - Snake council representative

## Values

The Deep-Root Kin value cunning, patience, and the ancient knowledge of the deep woods. They understand that being misunderstood by the mammalian factions can be an advantage, allowing them to operate in the shadows and preserve secrets that others might forget. They embrace their alien nature and see their differences as strengths rather than weaknesses.

## Role

The Deep-Root Kin serve as the keepers of forgotten lore and hidden paths in the Scarlet Wood. Their stealth and ambush tactics make them excellent scouts and guardians of the forest's most secret places. They often handle tasks requiring subtlety, translation of ancient or foreign texts, and navigation through the deepest, darkest parts of the Wood where others fear to tread.
