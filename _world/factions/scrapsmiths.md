## Overview

The Scrapsmiths are a faction known for their ingenuity and resourcefulness. Comprising primarily of small to medium-sized mammals such as raccoons, opossums, and foxes, they excel in scavenging materials and repurposing them into useful tools, gadgets, and inventions. Their creativity and adaptability make them masters of innovation in the forest.

## Notable Members

- **[Rook Gearclaw]({{ "/world/npcs/rook-gearclaw/" | relative_url }})** - Raccoon councilor and technical expert

## Values

The Scrapsmiths value ingenuity, adaptability, and the belief that nothing is truly waste—only materials waiting to be repurposed. They embrace change and innovation, seeing every problem as an opportunity to invent a clever solution. Their philosophy is that the best tools come not from pristine resources but from making the most of what's available, turning "trash" into treasure through creativity and skill.

## Role

The Scrapsmiths serve as the inventors, traders, and problem-solvers of the Scarlet Wood. They scavenge materials from human settlements and repurpose them into tools, traps, and devices that help the forestkin defend their home and improve their lives. Their technical expertise makes them invaluable for understanding and countering human technology, while their trading networks help distribute resources throughout the forest community.
