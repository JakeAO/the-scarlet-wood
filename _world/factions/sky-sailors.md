## Overview

The Sky-Sailors are a faction known for their mastery of flight and aerial navigation. Comprising primarily of birds such as hawks, owls, and eagles, they excel in scouting, transportation, and aerial combat. Their keen eyesight and swift movements make them invaluable for reconnaissance and rapid response in the forest.

## Notable Members

- **[Orven Taloncrest]({{ "/world/npcs/orven-taloncrest/" | relative_url }})** - Hawk, former Sky-Sailor leader and current Forest Guard captain

## Values

The Sky-Sailors value freedom, vigilance, and the strategic advantage that comes from seeing the bigger picture. They believe in swift action, clear communication, and maintaining an overview of threats before they arrive. Their perspective from above gives them a unique understanding of how the forest connects to the wider world, and they take pride in being the eyes and messengers of the Scarlet Wood.

## Role

The Sky-Sailors serve as the scouts, messengers, and aerial defenders of the Scarlet Wood. Their ability to move swiftly through the air makes them perfect for reconnaissance missions, carrying urgent messages between distant parts of the forest, and spotting threats before they reach the trees. Many Sky-Sailors also serve in the Forest Guard, where their aerial perspective and rapid response capabilities prove invaluable in protecting the Wood's borders.
