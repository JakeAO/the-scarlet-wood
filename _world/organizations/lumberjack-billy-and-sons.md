Lumberjack Billy & Sons is a family-owned logging operation run by [Billy Sr.]({{ "/world/enemies/billy-sr/" | relative_url }}) and his two sons, [Eli]({{ "/world/enemies/eli-billy/" | relative_url }}) and [Billy Jr.]({{ "/world/enemies/billy-jr/" | relative_url }}). The company works as subcontractors for [WoodCo]({{ "/world/organizations/woodco/" | relative_url }}), conducting logging operations and searching for "resonant samples" containing [latent natural magic]({{ "/info/campaign/world-of-lost-magic/" | relative_url }}).

## Overview

The family business is composed of three members: [Billy Sr.]({{ "/world/enemies/billy-sr/" | relative_url }}), the owner and operator who runs operations with traditional methods and a rough exterior; [Eli Billy]({{ "/world/enemies/eli-billy/" | relative_url }}), the oldest son and heir apparent who deeply respects his father's approach; and [Billy Jr.]({{ "/world/enemies/billy-jr/" | relative_url }}), the youngest son who advocates for modernization but is frequently dismissed. All three are large, muscular men skilled with axes, which they use both for logging and as weapons.

## Details

- **Leadership:** [Billy Sr.]({{ "/world/enemies/billy-sr/" | relative_url }}) owns and operates the company, set to eventually pass it to [Eli]({{ "/world/enemies/eli-billy/" | relative_url }})
- **Operations:** Conducts small-scale logging operations in forests near the Scarlet Wood under WoodCo contracts
- **Mission:** Hired by [WoodCo]({{ "/world/organizations/woodco/" | relative_url }}) to harvest timber and locate samples with magical properties
- **Equipment:** Uses traditional logging tools and pickup trucks; resists modernization despite [Billy Jr.]({{ "/world/enemies/billy-jr/" | relative_url }})'s suggestions
- **Discovered:** The PCs found their logging camp, stole documents revealing their connection to WoodCo, and destroyed their trailer office
