Billy Sr. is the human owner and operator of [Lumberjack Billy & Sons]({{ "/world/enemies/lumberjack-billy-and-sons/" | relative_url }}), a logging operation working under contract with [WoodCo]({{ "/world/enemies/woodco/" | relative_url }}). As patriarch of the family business, he leads logging operations with traditional methods and a no-nonsense approach.

## Overview

Billy Sr. runs the family logging business with his two sons, [Eli]({{ "/world/enemies/eli-billy/" | relative_url }}) and [Billy Jr.]({{ "/world/enemies/billy-jr/" | relative_url }}). He maintains a rough exterior when dealing with his sons, though he is slow to become truly angry. His traditional approach to logging has kept the business running for years.

## Appearance

- **Species:** Human
- **Notable Features:** Large, muscular build suited for logging work
- **Typical Attire:** Logger's work clothing and equipment

## Role and Position

- **Title/Position:** Owner and Operator
- **Affiliation:** [Lumberjack Billy & Sons]({{ "/world/organizations/lumberjack-billy-and-sons/" | relative_url }})
- **Responsibilities:** Leading logging operations, managing contracts with WoodCo, overseeing his sons' work

## Personality and Motivations

Billy Sr. treats his sons with a rough exterior, maintaining the gruff demeanor expected of a traditional logger. While slow to become truly angry, his preference for established methods over new technology puts him at odds with his younger son's ideas for modernizing the business.

## Relationships

- **Family:** Father of [Eli Billy]({{ "/world/enemies/eli-billy/" | relative_url }}) and [Billy Jr.]({{ "/world/enemies/billy-jr/" | relative_url }})
- **Allies:** [WoodCo]({{ "/world/organizations/woodco/" | relative_url }}) contractors who hire the family business
- **Rivals:** Forestkin of the Scarlet Wood who oppose the logging operations
