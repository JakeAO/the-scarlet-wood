Eli Billy is the oldest son of [Billy Sr.]({{ "/world/enemies/billy-sr/" | relative_url }}) and heir apparent to [Lumberjack Billy & Sons]({{ "/world/organizations/lumberjack-billy-and-sons/" | relative_url }}). Twenty years older than his younger brother, Eli embodies the traditional values and physical prowess his father expects in a logger.

## Overview

As the oldest son by 20 years, Eli is set to inherit the logging company when his father eventually retires—though that day seems far off. He deeply respects his father and shares his traditional approach to the logging business, often dismissing his younger brother's ideas for modernization.

## Appearance

- **Species:** Human
- **Notable Features:** Large, muscular build matching his father's physique
- **Typical Attire:** Logger's work clothing and equipment

## Role and Position

- **Title/Position:** Logger and Heir Apparent
- **Affiliation:** [Lumberjack Billy & Sons]({{ "/world/organizations/lumberjack-billy-and-sons/" | relative_url }})
- **Responsibilities:** Logging operations, following WoodCo work orders, learning to eventually take over the family business

## Personality and Motivations

Eli deeply respects his father and has adopted his traditional approach to logging. He believes [Billy Jr.]({{ "/world/enemies/billy-jr/" | relative_url }}) needs to toughen up and prove himself through physical strength and endurance rather than relying on technology or clever solutions. His confidence in inheriting the business reinforces his adherence to established methods.

## Relationships

- **Family:** Son of [Billy Sr.]({{ "/world/enemies/billy-sr/" | relative_url }}), older brother of [Billy Jr.]({{ "/world/enemies/billy-jr/" | relative_url }}) (by 20 years)
- **Allies:** [WoodCo]({{ "/world/organizations/woodco/" | relative_url }}) contractors, other members of the logging operation
- **Rivals:** Forestkin of the Scarlet Wood who oppose the logging operations
- **Notable Connections:** Frequently dismisses his younger brother's suggestions for modernizing operations
