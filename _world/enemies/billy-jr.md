Billy Jr. is the youngest son of [Billy Sr.]({{ "/world/enemies/billy-sr/" | relative_url }}) and works as part of the [Lumberjack Billy & Sons]({{ "/world/organizations/lumberjack-billy-and-sons/" | relative_url }}) operation. Twenty years younger than his older brother [Eli]({{ "/world/enemies/eli-billy/" | relative_url }}), Billy Jr. is smart but lacks the physical strength and toughness that define his family's traditional approach to logging.

## Overview

Billy Jr. was the only logger present when the PCs infiltrated the logging camp outside the Scarlet Wood. As a member of the family logging business, he works under contract for [WoodCo]({{ "/world/organizations/woodco/" | relative_url }}) to harvest timber and locate "resonant samples." Despite his intelligence, he struggles to gain respect from his father and brother, who frequently ignore his suggestions for modernizing operations with newer technology and streamlined processes.

## Appearance

- **Species:** Human
- **Typical Attire:** Logger's work clothing and equipment

## Role and Position

- **Title/Position:** Logger
- **Affiliation:** [Lumberjack Billy & Sons]({{ "/world/organizations/lumberjack-billy-and-sons/" | relative_url }})
- **Responsibilities:** Logging operations, site management, following WoodCo work orders

## Personality and Motivations

Billy Jr. is intelligent and forward-thinking, wanting to embrace newer technology and tools to streamline the logging processes. However, his lack of physical strength compared to his father and brother has left him feeling like he might not be cut out for the traditional logging business. His ideas for modernization are frequently ignored by [Billy Sr.]({{ "/world/enemies/billy-sr/" | relative_url }}) and [Eli]({{ "/world/enemies/eli-billy/" | relative_url }}), creating frustration and a sense that he doesn't fit the family mold.

## Relationships

- **Family:** Son of [Billy Sr.]({{ "/world/enemies/billy-sr/" | relative_url }}), younger brother of [Eli Billy]({{ "/world/enemies/eli-billy/" | relative_url }}) (by 20 years)
- **Allies:** Other members of the logging operation, WoodCo contractors
- **Rivals:** Forestkin of the Scarlet Wood who oppose the logging operations
- **Notable Connections:** Was present during the PCs' infiltration but unaware of their activities; ideas for modernization frequently dismissed by father and brother

## Campaign Involvement

Billy Jr. was at the logging camp during a crucial turning point:

- [Session 4]({{ "/sessions/session-04-the-logging-camp/" | relative_url }}) - Was the only logger present at the camp when the PCs infiltrated, searched the trailer, stole documents, and set the office on fire. His presence required the PCs to work stealthily.
