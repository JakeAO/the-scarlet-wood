Mistgleam is a spider and member of the [Deep-Root Kin]({{ "/world/factions/deep-root-kin/" | relative_url }}) who possesses the rare ability to read and translate human writing. This expertise proved crucial in revealing WoodCo's true intentions regarding [latent natural magic]({{ "/info/campaign/world-of-lost-magic/" | relative_url }}).

## Overview

As a member of the Deep-Root Kin, Mistgleam provided essential translation services for documents stolen from the logging camp. Their ability to interpret human writing uncovered WoodCo's search for "resonant samples" in forested areas.

## Appearance

- **Species:** Spider
- **Notable Features:** Multiple eyes suited for detailed reading and observation

## Role and Position

- **Title/Position:** Translator and Deep-Root Kin member
- **Affiliation:** [Deep-Root Kin]({{ "/world/factions/deep-root-kin/" | relative_url }})
- **Responsibilities:** Translates human documents, provides intelligence on human activities

## Relationships

- **Allies:** [Deep-Root Kin]({{ "/world/factions/deep-root-kin/" | relative_url }}) members including Creeve and Sablecoil, council members
- **Notable Connections:** Works alongside Rook Gearclaw on document analysis

## Campaign Involvement

Mistgleam's translation work was pivotal to understanding the threat:

- [Session 5]({{ "/sessions/session-05-proof-and-resolution/" | relative_url }}) - Translated portions of the stolen paperwork, revealing WoodCo was hiring subcontractors to locate "resonant samples" containing [latent natural magic]({{ "/info/campaign/world-of-lost-magic/" | relative_url }})
