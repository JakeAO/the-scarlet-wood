Foreman Bristleback is a beaver who represents the [Earth-Movers]({{ "/world/factions/earth-movers/" | relative_url }}) faction on the council, bringing their perspective on construction, engineering, and territorial defense to governance decisions.

## Overview

As the Earth-Movers' representative, Foreman Bristleback participates in council deliberations about threats to the Scarlet Wood. His faction's expertise in building and earth-moving makes their perspective valuable for defensive preparations. While fiercely protective of the Wood and the forestkin way of life, his tendency toward caution and traditionalism can sometimes slow decisive action.

## Appearance

- **Species:** Beaver
- **Notable Features:** Very large and stocky, even for a beaver; build suited for physical labor and engineering work
- **Typical Attire:** Work gear appropriate for a foreman, adapted for council sessions

## Role and Position

- **Title/Position:** Foreman and Council Representative
- **Affiliation:** [Earth-Movers]({{ "/world/factions/earth-movers/" | relative_url }})
- **Responsibilities:** Represents Earth-Movers in council decisions, likely oversees construction and engineering projects for the Scarlet Wood

## Personality and Motivations

Foreman Bristleback is fiercely protective of the Scarlet Wood and the forestkin way of life, bringing passion to his defense of tradition and established practices. However, this devotion to tradition makes him overly reliant on "the way things were" or "have always been," sometimes resisting necessary change or innovation. He tends toward inaction in situations where quick decisions may be required, preferring to deliberate and consult precedent before committing to a course of action. This cautious approach can be both a strength and a weakness in council deliberations.

## Relationships

- **Allies:** Other council members, [Earth-Movers]({{ "/world/factions/earth-movers/" | relative_url }}) faction members
- **Notable Connections:** Part of the council overseeing the PCs' activities
