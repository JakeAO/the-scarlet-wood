Marla Mossfur is an ancient mouse who represents the [Moss-Fur Clan]({{ "/world/factions/moss-fur-clan/" | relative_url }}) in council sessions, participating in key decisions about the Scarlet Wood's response to external threats.

## Overview

As a council representative for the Moss-Fur Clan, Marla participates in deliberations about threats to the Scarlet Wood and helps determine appropriate responses. She was present for both the abbreviated council that judged the PCs and the full council that decided how to handle the WoodCo threat.

## Appearance

- **Species:** Mouse
- **Notable Features:** Small stature typical of the Moss-Fur Clan
- **Typical Attire:** Council attire appropriate for formal deliberations

## Role and Position

- **Title/Position:** Council Representative
- **Affiliation:** [Moss-Fur Clan]({{ "/world/factions/moss-fur-clan/" | relative_url }})
- **Responsibilities:** Represents Moss-Fur Clan interests in council decisions, participates in governance of the Scarlet Wood

## Relationships

- **Allies:** Other council members, [Moss-Fur Clan]({{ "/world/factions/moss-fur-clan/" | relative_url }}) including Matron Hilda
- **Notable Connections:** Part of the council that oversees the PCs' missions

## Campaign Involvement

Marla has participated in council decisions affecting the PCs:

- [Session 3]({{ "/sessions/session-03-the-abbreviated-council/" | relative_url }}) - Questioned the PCs about their unauthorized attack on hunters during the abbreviated council session
- [Session 5]({{ "/sessions/session-05-proof-and-resolution/" | relative_url }}) - Participated in the full council that decided to send the PCs to stop Lumberjack Billy & Sons
