Matron Hilda is a fox and elder of the [Moss-Fur Clan]({{ "/world/factions/moss-fur-clan/" | relative_url }}), holding significant authority within both her faction and the broader council structure of the Scarlet Wood.

## Overview

As a matron and elder, Hilda represents the Moss-Fur Clan's leadership and wisdom in council deliberations. Her presence at the full council session suggests she holds authority beyond the standard council representative role filled by Marla Mossfur.

## Appearance

- **Species:** Fox
- **Notable Features:** Bearing and presence befitting an elder
- **Typical Attire:** Elder's attire appropriate for formal council sessions

## Role and Position

- **Title/Position:** Matron and Elder
- **Affiliation:** [Moss-Fur Clan]({{ "/world/factions/moss-fur-clan/" | relative_url }})
- **Responsibilities:** Provides wisdom and leadership for the Moss-Fur Clan, participates in major council decisions

## Relationships

- **Allies:** [Moss-Fur Clan]({{ "/world/factions/moss-fur-clan/" | relative_url }}) members including Marla Mossfur, other council elders
- **Notable Connections:** Part of the council leadership overseeing responses to major threats

## Campaign Involvement

Matron Hilda participated in the critical full council session:

- [Session 5]({{ "/sessions/session-05-proof-and-resolution/" | relative_url }}) - Attended the full council meeting where the PCs presented evidence of WoodCo's activities and the council decided on their response strategy
