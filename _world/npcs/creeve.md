Creeve is a snake who represents the [Deep-Root Kin]({{ "/world/factions/deep-root-kin/" | relative_url }}) in council proceedings, bringing their faction's perspective on underground matters and the deeper mysteries of the Scarlet Wood.

## Overview

As a Deep-Root Kin representative, Creeve participates in major council deliberations. The Deep-Root Kin's connection to the earth and ancient knowledge makes their council representation particularly important for matters involving the forest's magical properties.

## Appearance

- **Species:** Snake
- **Notable Features:** Serpentine form suited for navigating underground spaces

## Role and Position

- **Title/Position:** Council Representative
- **Affiliation:** [Deep-Root Kin]({{ "/world/factions/deep-root-kin/" | relative_url }})
- **Responsibilities:** Represents Deep-Root Kin interests in council decisions

## Relationships

- **Allies:** [Deep-Root Kin]({{ "/world/factions/deep-root-kin/" | relative_url }}) members including Sablecoil and Mistgleam, other council members
- **Notable Connections:** Part of the council overseeing responses to threats against the Scarlet Wood

## Campaign Involvement

Creeve attended the critical full council session:

- [Session 5]({{ "/sessions/session-05-proof-and-resolution/" | relative_url }}) - Participated in the full council meeting where the faction representatives debated whether to pursue WoodCo directly or retaliate against Lumberjack Billy & Sons
