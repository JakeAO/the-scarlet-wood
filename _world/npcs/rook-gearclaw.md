Rook Gearclaw is a tiny raccoon who serves as councilor for the [Scrapsmiths]({{ "/world/factions/scrapsmiths/" | relative_url }}), bringing technical expertise to council deliberations. He proved instrumental in analyzing the documents stolen from the logging camp, identifying WoodCo's coordinated operations.

## Overview

As a Scrapsmith councilor, Rook combines political authority with technical knowledge, particularly regarding human documents, maps, and equipment. He assisted the PCs in interpreting stolen paperwork that revealed the scope of WoodCo's activities.

## Appearance

- **Species:** Raccoon
- **Notable Features:** Dexterous paws suited for detailed work
- **Typical Attire:** Likely wears Scrapsmith tools and gear along with council attire

## Role and Position

- **Title/Position:** Councilor, Crafter
- **Affiliation:** [Scrapsmiths]({{ "/world/factions/scrapsmiths/" | relative_url }})
- **Responsibilities:** Represents Scrapsmith interests on the council, analyzes technical matters including human documents and equipment

## Personality and Motivations

Rook demonstrates analytical skills and willingness to assist with investigations. His technical expertise makes him a valuable resource for understanding human activities and technology.

## Relationships

- **Allies:** Other council members, [Scrapsmiths]({{ "/world/factions/scrapsmiths/" | relative_url }}) faction members
- **Notable Connections:** Consulted by the PCs for document analysis, works alongside Mistgleam on translation efforts

## Campaign Involvement

Rook has provided crucial analysis for the PCs' investigation:

- [Session 3]({{ "/sessions/session-03-the-abbreviated-council/" | relative_url }}) - Questioned the PCs during the abbreviated council session about the risks of their actions
- [Session 5]({{ "/sessions/session-05-proof-and-resolution/" | relative_url }}) - Analyzed the stolen maps and letters, identifying them as part of a coordinated WoodCo operation; his faction provided the spike trap used in the convoy ambush
