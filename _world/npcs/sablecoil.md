Sablecoil is a snake who represents the [Deep-Root Kin]({{ "/world/factions/deep-root-kin/" | relative_url }}) in council proceedings, joining Creeve in bringing their faction's voice to major decisions affecting the Scarlet Wood.

## Overview

Sablecoil serves alongside Creeve as a Deep-Root Kin representative on the council. The presence of multiple snake representatives suggests the Deep-Root Kin hold significant influence in the governance structure of the Scarlet Wood.

## Appearance

- **Species:** Snake
- **Notable Features:** Serpentine form adapted for underground environments

## Role and Position

- **Title/Position:** Council Representative
- **Affiliation:** [Deep-Root Kin]({{ "/world/factions/deep-root-kin/" | relative_url }})
- **Responsibilities:** Represents Deep-Root Kin interests in council decisions

## Relationships

- **Allies:** [Deep-Root Kin]({{ "/world/factions/deep-root-kin/" | relative_url }}) members including Creeve and Mistgleam, other council members
- **Notable Connections:** Part of the council leadership making strategic decisions for the Scarlet Wood

## Campaign Involvement

Sablecoil participated in the critical full council session:

- [Session 5]({{ "/sessions/session-05-proof-and-resolution/" | relative_url }}) - Attended the full council meeting that decided to monitor WoodCo movements while sending the PCs to stop Lumberjack Billy & Sons
