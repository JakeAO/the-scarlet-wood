Orven Taloncrest is a hawk who serves as captain of the Forest Guard, commanding trainee patrols along the borders of the Scarlet Wood. A former leader of the [Sky-Sailors]({{ "/world/factions/sky-sailors/" | relative_url }}), he brings experience and authority to his role protecting the forest from external threats.

## Overview

Orven transitioned from leading the Sky-Sailors to commanding the Forest Guard, where he oversees compulsory service training for young forestkin. He demonstrates protective instincts toward his trainees while maintaining military discipline and tactical awareness.

## Appearance

- **Species:** Hawk
- **Notable Features:** Keen eyes typical of a raptor, commanding presence
- **Typical Attire:** Forest Guard uniform and equipment

## Role and Position

- **Title/Position:** Forest Guard Captain
- **Affiliation:** Forest Guard, formerly [Sky-Sailors]({{ "/world/factions/sky-sailors/" | relative_url }})
- **Responsibilities:** Commands border patrols, trains young forestkin in Forest Guard service, coordinates responses to external threats

## Personality and Motivations

Orven balances protective concern for his trainees with the need to prepare them for real dangers. He shows willingness to take responsibility for his subordinates' actions while maintaining clear orders and expectations. His leadership style emphasizes caution and proper reconnaissance before engagement.

## Relationships

- **Allies:** Council representatives from all factions, Forest Guard forces
- **Notable Connections:** Directly commands the PC party, maintains communication with faction leaders

## Campaign Involvement

Orven has been central to the PCs' involvement in investigating the logging threat:

- [Session 1]({{ "/sessions/session-01-northern-border-patrol/" | relative_url }}) - Led the PCs on border patrol where they discovered clear-cutting, ordered investigation while warning them to avoid human contact
- [Session 3]({{ "/sessions/session-03-the-abbreviated-council/" | relative_url }}) - Attempted to take responsibility for the PCs' unauthorized attack on hunters before the council
- [Session 5]({{ "/sessions/session-05-proof-and-resolution/" | relative_url }}) - Coordinated scout deployments and verified the PCs' infiltration didn't result in human casualties
