This section contains the official Utopia TTRPG rules for creating custom creatures, monsters, and NPCs.

*   [Creature Kits]({{ "/info/utopia-rules/custom-creature-creation/02-kits/" | relative_url }}) - Stackable upgrades like Tank, Evasive, and Aggressive to modify creatures.
