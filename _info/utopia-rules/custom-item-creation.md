# Custom Item Creation Rules

This section contains the official Utopia TTRPG rules for crafting custom items, weapons, and armor. These rules allow players and GMs to design equipment from the ground up, calculating costs, crafting times, and rarity based on chosen features.

*   [Head Armor]({{ "/info/utopia-rules/custom-item-creation/06-head-armor/" | relative_url }}) - Helmets and cowls.
