# CREATURE BODIES

Exactly one creature body must be chosen for each creature design. If an option is written in a Option/Option/Option format, any single option may be chosen at no change to other stats.

## Elemental

*   **Base DR:** 17
*   **Stats:** 20 SHP, 45 DHP, 20 Stamina
*   **Defenses:** 5 Physical Defense, 0 in all other defenses
*   **Subtraits:** 4 Speed, 3 Dexterity, 5 Awareness, 5 Portrayal, 2 Stunt, 4 Appeal, 1 in all other subtraits
*   **Avoidance:** 4d12 Dodge, 1d4 Block

**Passive Features**
*   This creature does not need to breathe, eat, drink water, or sleep. It cannot be inflicted with points of Fatigue or Unconsciousness.
*   This creature hovers above the ground up to 1 meter and ignores damage dealt from falling and can levitate above liquids as if it was solid ground. Its **Land travel** is equal to its Speed score plus 2.
*   This creature's Land travel cannot be reduced.
*   This creature ignores **Energy/Heat/Chill** damage.

**Innate Actions**
*   **Attack (2 Turn Actions):**
    *   **Range:** 5/10 meters (Ranged)
    *   **Damage:** 2d8 + [Portrayal Mod] **Energy/Heat/Chill** damage.
    *   *Effect:* This creature may spend up to 3 additional turn actions, dealing an additional **2d8 damage** of the same type per additional turn action.

## Beast

*   **Base DR:** 5
*   **Stats:** 10 SHP, 5 DHP, 10 Stamina
*   **Defenses:** 1 of each defense
*   **Subtraits:** 2 Speed, 2 Dexterity, 2 Power, 3 Awareness, 1 in all other subtraits
*   **Avoidance:** 3d12 Dodge, 2d4 Block

**Passive Features**
*   When this creature becomes the target of an effect, it may spend **3 stamina** to take the **Travel** action using interrupt actions as if they were turn actions.

**Innate Actions**
*   **Attack (2 Turn Actions):**
    *   **Range:** 0 meters (Melee)
    *   **Damage:** 2d8 + [Power Mod] **Physical** damage.

**Harvest & Loot**
*   **Base Loot:** 1d6 material components
*   **Harvest Test:** Engineering (TD = Half DR, rounded up)
*   **Success Loot:** 1d8 material components and 1d4 refinement components.

## Humanoid

*   **Base DR:** 5
*   **Stats:** 10 SHP, 10 DHP, 5 Stamina
*   **Defenses:** 1 of each defense
*   **Subtraits:** 2 Speed, 2 Dexterity, 2 Power, 2 Engineering, 2 Memory, 2 Awareness, 2 Portrayal, 2 Language, 1 in all other subtraits
*   **Avoidance:** 2d12 Dodge, 2d4 Block

**Innate Actions**
*   **Attack (2 Turn Actions):**
    *   **Range:** 0 meters (Melee)
    *   **Damage:** 1d8 + [Power Mod] **Physical** damage.
    *   *Effect:* This creature may spend up to 3 additional turn actions, dealing an additional **2d8 Physical damage** per additional turn action.

**Harvest & Loot**
*   **Base Loot:** 1d4 **Crude** material components
*   **Harvest Test:** Engineering (TD = Quarter DR, rounded up)
*   **Success Loot:** 1d4 refinement components.

## Construct

*   **Base DR:** 5
*   **Stats:** 15 SHP, 5 DHP, 5 Stamina
*   **Defenses:** 4 Physical Defense, 2 Energy Defense, 1 Chill Defense, 0 in all other defenses
*   **Subtraits:** 2 Speed, 2 Power, 4 Fortitude, 2 Resolve, 1 in all other subtraits
*   **Avoidance:** 1d12 Dodge, 1d4 Block

**Passive Features**
*   This creature does not need to breathe, eat, drink water, or sleep. It cannot be inflicted with points of Fatigue or Unconsciousness.
*   If this creature is being controlled by a caster, it ignores Psyche damage dealt to it. Instead, **Psyche** damage is dealt to the caster.

**Innate Actions**
*   **Attack (2 Turn Actions):**
    *   **Range:** 0 meters (Melee)
    *   **Damage:** 1d8 + [Power Mod] **Physical** damage.
    *   *Effect:* This creature may spend up to 3 additional turn actions, dealing an additional **2d8 Physical damage** per additional turn action.

**Harvest & Loot**
*   **Base Loot:** 1d4 material components
*   **Harvest Test:** Engineering (TD = Quarter DR, rounded up)
*   **Success Loot:** 1d10 material components and 1d4 power components.
*   *Note:* If this creature was created due to a spell, its harvestable component rarity and quantities cannot be higher than those used to create it.

## Draconic

*   **Base DR:** 50
*   **Stats:** 40 SHP, 45 DHP, 35 Stamina
*   **Defenses:** 19 Physical Defense, 9 of each other defense
*   **Subtraits:** 3 Speed, 2 Dexterity, 6 Power, 6 Fortitude, 6 Resolve, 3 Portrayal, 3 Appeal, 2 in all other subtraits
*   **Avoidance:** 4d12 Dodge, 4d4 Block

**Passive Features**
*   This creature ignores **Energy/Heat/Chill** damage.

**Innate Actions**
*   **Attack (2 Turn Actions):**
    *   **Range:** 2 meters (Melee)
    *   **Damage:** 6d12 **Physical** damage.
*   **Breath Attack (3 Turn Actions, 10 Stamina):**
    *   **Range:** 90 degree cone, 5 meters
    *   **Damage:** 4d10 **Energy/Heat/Chill** damage.

**Harvest & Loot**
*   **Base Loot:** 1d4 material components
*   **Harvest Test:** Engineering (TD = Half DR, rounded up)
*   **Success Loot:** 1d10 material components, 1d8 refinement components, and 1d6 power components.

## Abomination

*   **Base DR:** 3
*   **Stats:** 5 SHP, 5 DHP, 5 Stamina
*   **Defenses:** 5 Physical Defense, 3 in each other defense
*   **Subtraits:** 2 Dexterity, 2 Power, 2 Fortitude, 1 in all other subtraits
*   **Avoidance:** 1d12 Dodge, 1d4 Block

**Passive Features**
*   This creature gains a point of favor on tests made to resist being influenced or forced to commit to an action.

**Innate Actions**
*   **Attack (2 Turn Actions):**
    *   **Range:** 0 meters (Melee)
    *   **Damage:** 1d8 **Physical** damage.

**Harvest & Loot**
*   **Base Loot:** 1d6 material components
*   **Harvest Test:** Engineering (TD = Half DR, rounded up)
*   **Success Loot:** 1d10 material components, 1d10 refinement components, and 1d10 power components.
