# MARTIAL CLASSES

Martial classes offer general combat capabilities, increasing damage and often offering a ranged attack of some type.

A creature can have up to one martial class.

## Brute (+8 DR)
*   **Stats:** SHP increases by **25**, DHP increases by **15**.
*   **Defenses:** Physical defense increases by **4**; Energy, Heat, and Chill defense each increase by **1**.
*   **Subtraits:** Power increases by **4**; Speed and Fortitude each increase by **2**.
*   **Avoidance:** Block Rating increases by **1d4**.

**Passive Features**
*   When a melee attack this creature makes deals enough damage to reduce its target's DHP to 0, it may retarget the remaining damage to another creature within range.
*   This creature gains an additional point of favor on tests made to **grapple**.

**Innate Actions**
*   **Attack (1 Turn Action):**
    *   **Range:** 0 meters (Melee)
    *   **Damage:** 4d4 **Physical** damage.

**Equipment (Reduce XP if dropped)**
*   Dagger (-80 XP)
*   Garments (-20 XP)

## Martialist (+15 DR)
*   **Stats:** SHP increases by **35**, DHP increases by **30**, Stamina increases by **10**.
*   **Defenses:** Physical defense increases by **4**; each other defense other than Psyche increases by **2**.
*   **Subtraits:** Dexterity and Power increase by **5**; Speed increases by **3**, Fortitude increases by **2**.
*   **Avoidance:** Dodge Rating increases by **2d12**, Block Rating increases by **2d4**.

**Passive Features**
*   When a melee attack this creature makes deals enough damage to reduce its target's DHP to 0, it may retarget the remaining damage to another creature within range.
*   This creature gains an additional point of favor on tests made to **grapple**.
*   When this creature takes the **Travel** action, it may spend **3 stamina** to add its **Stunt score** to its Land/Water/Air travel for the rest of the action.
*   When this creature takes the **Block** action against a melee attack, they may spend **1 additional interrupt action** and **5 stamina** to roll its melee weapon's attack damage rather than its Block Rating.

**Innate Actions**
*   **Attack (3 Turn Actions):**
    *   **Range:** 2 meters (Melee)
    *   **Damage:** 8d8 **Physical** damage.
*   **Attack (3 Turn Actions):**
    *   **Range:** 20/40 meters (Ranged)
    *   **Damage:** 4d10 + [Dexterity Mod] **Physical** damage.

**Equipment (Reduce XP if dropped)**
*   Claymore (-60 XP)
*   Longbow (-64 XP)
*   Body Armor (-80 XP)

## Militant (+30 DR)
*   **Stats:** SHP increases by **70**, DHP increases by **60**, Stamina increases by **20**.
*   **Defenses:** Physical defense increases by **7**, Energy defense increases by **5**; Heat and Chill defenses each increase by **4**.
*   **Subtraits:** Dexterity and Power increase by **8**; Speed increases by **6**, Fortitude increases by **6**, and Resolve increases **4**.
*   **Avoidance:** Dodge Rating increases by **5d12**, Block Rating increases by **6d4**.

**Passive Features**
*   When a melee attack this creature makes deals enough damage to reduce its target's DHP to 0, it may retarget the remaining damage to another creature within range.
*   This creature gains an additional point of favor on tests made to **grapple**.
*   When this creature takes the **Travel** action, it may spend **3 stamina** to add its **Stunt score** to its Land/Water/Air travel for the rest of the action.
*   Whenever another creature that this one can sense takes the **Attack** action, this creature may spend **8 stamina** to take the **Attack** action using interrupt actions as if they were turn actions.

**Innate Actions**
*   **Attack (1 Turn Action):**
    *   **Range:** 1 meter (Melee)
    *   **Damage:** 4d4 **Energy** damage.
*   **Attack (2 Turn Actions):**
    *   **Range:** 20/40 meters (Ranged)
    *   **Damage:** 4d8 **Energy** damage.

**Equipment (Reduce XP if dropped)**
*   Force Blade (-248 XP)
*   Force Arbalest (-280 XP)
*   Gauntlets (-80 XP)
*   Power Body (-280 XP)

## Mighty (+65 DR)
*   **Stats:** SHP and DHP each increase by **140**, Stamina increases by **35**.
*   **Defenses:** Physical defense increases by **16**, Energy defense increases by **14**; Heat and Chill defenses each increase by **12**, Psyche defense increases by **10**.
*   **Subtraits:** Power increases by **15**; Speed and Fortitude each increase by **10**; Dexterity increases by **8**; Resolve and Awareness each increase by **7**; Stunt increases by **4**; Portrayal, Appeal, and Language each increase by **2**.
*   **Avoidance:** Dodge Rating increases by **4d12**, Block Rating increases by **8d4**.

**Passive Features**
*   This creature has **9 turn actions** and **3 interrupt actions** per turn.
*   When a melee attack this creature makes deals enough damage to reduce its target's DHP to 0, it may retarget the remaining damage to another creature within range.
*   This creature gains an additional point of favor on tests made to **grapple**.
*   When this creature makes a melee attack, it may choose to target **each creature within the attack’s range** rather than a single target. Attacks made this way deal half damage to each creature.
*   When this creature takes the **Travel** action, it may spend **3 stamina** to add its **Stunt score** to its Land/Water/Air travel for the rest of the action.
*   Whenever another creature that this one can sense takes the **Attack** action, this creature may spend **8 stamina** to take the **Attack** action using interrupt actions as if they were turn actions.
*   When this creature makes an attack, it may spend **3 stamina** to reduce the number of turn actions required by **1**, minimum of 4 turn actions. It may also spend **5 stamina** to do the same with a minimum of 2 turn actions, or **7 stamina** with a minimum of 1 turn action. It may do so any number of times per attack.

**Innate Actions**
*   **Attack (6 Turn Actions):**
    *   **Range:** 2 meters (Melee)
    *   **Damage:** 10d10 + [Power Mod] **Physical**, ignores defenses and SHP.
*   **Attack (2 Turn Actions):**
    *   **Range:** 10/20 meters (Ranged)
    *   **Damage:** 3d10 **Physical** + 4d8 **Energy**.

**Equipment (Reduce XP if dropped)**
*   Tremor Hammer (-1872 XP)
*   Shatter Gat (-816 XP)
*   Imperial Chestpiece (-880 XP)
*   Power Helm (-256 XP)
*   Gauntlets (-80 XP)
*   Greaves (-80 XP)
