# KITS

Each kit can be used any number of times, increasing the creature's Difficulty Rating (DR) and giving it a number of stackable features.

## Defensive (+2 DR)
*   **Stats:** SHP and DHP are each increased by **5**.
*   **Defenses:** Any single defense increases by **4**.
*   **Avoidance:** Either Block Rating is increased by **1d4** or Dodge Rating is increased by **1d12**.

## Tank (+4 DR)
*   **Stats:** SHP and DHP each increase by **10**.
*   **Defenses:** Physical and Energy defense each increase by **3**, each other defense increases by **1**.
*   **Subtraits:** Power increases by **1**, Fortitude increases by **3**.
*   **Avoidance:** Block Rating increases by **1d4**.

## Evasive (+4 DR)
*   **Stats:** SHP and DHP each increase by **5**, Stamina increases by **10**.
*   **Subtraits:** Speed and Dexterity each increase by **2**.
*   **Avoidance:** Dodge Rating increases by **1d12**.
*   **Travel:** Land/Water/Air travel increases by **2**.
*   **Features:** This creature gains an additional point of favor on tests made to resist spell effects.

## Aggressive (+4 DR)
*   **Stats:** DHP increases by **5**, Stamina increases by **15**.
*   **Subtraits:** Dexterity increases by **1**, Power increases by **3**.
*   **Travel:** Land/Water/Air travel increases by **2**.
*   **Features:**
    *   When this creature takes the **Travel** action, this creature may spend **3 times X stamina**, whereas X is the number of stacks of this kit the creature has. If it does, its Land/Water/Air travel increases by its **Stunt times X** for the rest of the action.
    *   When this creature makes an **Attack**, it may spend **double X turn actions** and spend **12 times X stamina**. If it does, it instead attacks a target at random and deals **X + 1 times as much damage**, whereas X is the number of stacks of this kit it has.

## Magus (+4 DR)
*   **Stats:** Stamina increases by **20**.
*   **Subtraits:** Resolve increases by **4**.
*   **Features:** The cost of spells this creature casts are reduced by an additional **3 stamina**, minimum of 1.

## Doppelganger (+6 DR)
*   **Stats:** SHP increases by **5**, DHP increases by **20**, Stamina increases by **5**.
*   **Defenses:** Each defense increases by **1**.
*   **Subtraits:** Stunt increases by **1**; Portrayal, Appeal, and Language each increase by **2**.
*   **Features:** This creature gains **2 additional points of favor** on tests made to disguise itself or keep its identity unknown, as well as **2 additional points of favor** on tests made to mimic the actions of another creature.

## Powerful (+20 DR)
*   **Stats:** SHP and DHP each increase by **40**, Stamina increases by **20**.
*   **Defenses:** Physical defense increases by **12**, Energy defense increases by **10**, Heat and Chill defense each increase by **9**, and Psyche defense increases by **5**.
*   **Subtraits:** Portrayal, Stunt, Appeal, and Language each increase by **1**, each other subtrait increases by **2**.
*   **Avoidance:** Dodge Rating increases by **2d12**, Block Rating increases by **2d4**.
*   **Features:** This creature gains an additional point of favor on tests made to resist spell effects.
