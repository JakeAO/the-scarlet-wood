# INNATE CLASSES

These rankings aren't due to any form of equipment or special training, but rather a feature of the creature's biology.

A creature may have up to 1 of each innate class.

## Skyborn (+2 DR)
*   **Stats:** Stamina increases by **10**, DHP increases by **5**.
*   **Subtraits:** Stunt increases by **3**.
*   **Travel:** Its **Air travel** becomes equal to its **Speed score**; its **Land travel** becomes equal to **half its Speed score**, rounded up.

## Seaborn (+3 DR)
*   **Stats:** SHP and DHP each increase by **5**.
*   **Subtraits:** Speed increases by **1**, Fortitude increases by **1**.
*   **Travel:** Its **Water travel** becomes equal to its **Speed score**.

**Passive Features**
*   This creature is able to breathe in both air and water.

## Untouchable (+25 DR)
*   **Stats:** SHP and DHP each increase by **50**, Stamina increases by **25**.
*   **Defenses:** Energy, Heat, Chill, and Psyche defense each increase by **5**.
*   **Subtraits:** Fortitude increases by **6**, each other subtrait increases by **3**.

**Passive Features**
*   This creature ignores all **Physical damage** dealt to it.

## Interchangeable (+X DR)
This class allows for high customization, scaling with the chosen DR value (X).

*   **Stats:** Total sum of SHP, DHP and Stamina increases by **X times 5**.
*   **Subtraits:** Subtraits increase by a total of **X**, distributed in any fashion.
*   **Talents & Quirks:**
    *   This creature gains a number of features from talents and quirks.
    *   It may use **X talent points**, plus an additional **3 points per 10 DR** this class gives. (e.g., If X is 15, it gains 18 points. If X is 28, it gains 34 points).
    *   It may treat quirks as if they were talents, treating quirk points as talent points.
    *   If a talent stacks, such as increasing the creature's defense, it may be taken multiple times. Talents do not need to be taken in their original order.
*   **Equipment:**
    *   This creature gains the effects from items based on their value.
    *   The total sum of value for all items granted by this class may be, at most, equal to **X squared** (X times X).
    *   The amount of XP reduced from receiving an item is equal to the item's value.
