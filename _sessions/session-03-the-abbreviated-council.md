The PCs appeared before an abbreviated council session at the core of the Scarlet Wood to answer for their unauthorized attack on the human hunters. Representatives from the [Moss-Fur Clan]({{ "/world/factions/moss-fur-clan/" | relative_url }}), [Scrapsmiths]({{ "/world/factions/scrapsmiths/" | relative_url }}), and [Earth-Movers]({{ "/world/factions/earth-movers/" | relative_url }}) questioned the trainees about the risks of human retaliation. Despite the breach of orders, the council issued a lenient judgment and assigned the PCs a dangerous scouting mission outside the forest.

## Overview

The PCs faced council judgment for disobeying orders and attacking humans. The council assigned them a reconnaissance mission to identify the loggers and their plans, with strict orders to avoid further engagement.

## Key Characters

- **[Orven Taloncrest]({{ "/world/npcs/orven-taloncrest/" | relative_url }})** - Hawk, Forest Guard captain who attempted to take responsibility for the PCs' actions
- **[Marla Mossfur]({{ "/world/npcs/marla-mossfur/" | relative_url }})** - Mouse, [Moss-Fur Clan]({{ "/world/factions/moss-fur-clan/" | relative_url }}) representative
- **[Rook Gearclaw]({{ "/world/npcs/rook-gearclaw/" | relative_url }})** - Raccoon, [Scrapsmiths]({{ "/world/factions/scrapsmiths/" | relative_url }}) councilor
- **[Foreman Bristleback]({{ "/world/npcs/foreman-bristleback/" | relative_url }})** - Beaver, [Earth-Movers]({{ "/world/factions/earth-movers/" | relative_url }}) representative

## Details

- The PCs participated in an abbreviated council session after other squads had given testimony throughout the day
- The council questioned the PCs about their unauthorized attack on the hunters and the potential for human retaliation
- [Orven Taloncrest]({{ "/world/npcs/orven-taloncrest/" | relative_url }}) attempted to take responsibility for the PCs' actions
- The council issued a lenient judgment, acknowledging the PCs' youth and recognizing their aptitude for dangerous assignments
- As both punishment and reward, the PCs were assigned a scouting mission outside the Scarlet Wood to identify the loggers and learn their plans
- The PCs were explicitly ordered not to engage with any humans during this reconnaissance mission
