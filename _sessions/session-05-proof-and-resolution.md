The PCs returned to the Scarlet Wood with stolen documents and presented their evidence to the council. With help from [Rook Gearclaw]({{ "/world/npcs/rook-gearclaw/" | relative_url }}) and [Mistgleam]({{ "/world/npcs/mistgleam/" | relative_url }}), they revealed that [WoodCo]({{ "/world/organizations/woodco/" | relative_url }}) was hiring subcontractors like [Lumberjack Billy & Sons]({{ "/world/organizations/lumberjack-billy-and-sons/" | relative_url }}) to locate "resonant samples" containing [latent natural magic]({{ "/info/campaign/world-of-lost-magic/" | relative_url }}). The council debated their response and ultimately deployed the PCs to intercept the logging convoy, where they successfully disabled one vehicle using a spike trap.

## Overview

The PCs consulted faction experts to interpret stolen documents, presented evidence of WoodCo's magical resource extraction to the full council, and prepared to intercept the loggers. The session culminated in a successful ambush on the logging convoy.

## Details

- The PCs returned to the Scarlet Wood and reported their findings to [Orven Taloncrest]({{ "/world/npcs/orven-taloncrest/" | relative_url }})
- [Orven]({{ "/world/npcs/orven-taloncrest/" | relative_url }}) dispatched scouts to verify that no humans were harmed during the infiltration and to monitor human movements
- The PCs consulted [Rook Gearclaw]({{ "/world/npcs/rook-gearclaw/" | relative_url }}) and [Mistgleam]({{ "/world/npcs/mistgleam/" | relative_url }}) to interpret the stolen documents
- [Rook]({{ "/world/npcs/rook-gearclaw/" | relative_url }}) identified that the maps and letters were part of a coordinated [WoodCo]({{ "/world/organizations/woodco/" | relative_url }}) operation
- [Mistgleam]({{ "/world/npcs/mistgleam/" | relative_url }}) translated portions of the paperwork, revealing that WoodCo was hiring subcontractors to locate "resonant samples" in forested areas
- A full council convened with representatives from all factions
- The PCs presented evidence that [Lumberjack Billy & Sons]({{ "/world/organizations/lumberjack-billy-and-sons/" | relative_url }}) were subcontractors working for WoodCo, and that WoodCo was seeking [latent natural magic]({{ "/info/campaign/world-of-lost-magic/" | relative_url }})
- The council debated whether to pursue WoodCo directly or retaliate against Lumberjack Billy & Sons
- The council resolved to monitor WoodCo movements, deploy scouts, prepare anti-vehicle defenses, and send the PCs to stop Lumberjack Billy & Sons
- After preparation, the PCs learned that a new WoodCo-associated human had joined the loggers
- The PCs intercepted the logging convoy en route to the Scarlet Wood
- Using a [Scrap-Smith]({{ "/world/factions/scrapsmiths/" | relative_url }}) spike trap, they successfully disabled one of two pickup trucks, initiating a confrontation to be continued in Session 6
