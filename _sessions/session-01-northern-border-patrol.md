The PCs, youthful [forestkin]({{ "/info/campaign/denizens-of-the-wood/" | relative_url }}) from multiple factions, began their compulsory Forest Guard service under the command of [Orven Taloncrest]({{ "/world/npcs/orven-taloncrest/" | relative_url }}). During their first patrol along the northern edge of the Scarlet Wood, the trainees discovered approximately one acre of forest that had been clear-cut, marking the beginning of a larger investigation into human activity near their home.

## Overview

Forest Guard trainees conducted patrol and maintenance duties along the northern perimeter and discovered evidence of illegal logging. Their captain ordered them to investigate while avoiding contact with nearby human campers.

## Key Characters

- **[Orven Taloncrest]({{ "/world/npcs/orven-taloncrest/" | relative_url }})** - Hawk, former [Sky-Sailor]({{ "/world/factions/sky-sailors/" | relative_url }}) leader and current Forest Guard captain who commanded the trainee patrol

## Details

- The PCs participated in Forest Guard service, conducting scouting, maintenance, and perimeter documentation along the northern edge of the Scarlet Wood
- During patrol, the trainees discovered a roughly one-acre section of forest that had been clear-cut
- Orven ordered the trainees to split into small groups to investigate the site and identify evidence of who was responsible
- Orven explicitly warned the trainees to avoid a small group of humans camped northeast of the clearing
