The party returns to the Scarlet Wood bearing both victory and tragedy—Basil's body and crucial evidence from the [Lumberjack Billy & Sons]({{ "/world/organizations/lumberjack-billy-and-sons/" | relative_url }}) crew. After an emergency council meeting and analysis of the recovered cell phone, the Wood learns that [WoodCo]({{ "/world/organizations/woodco/" | relative_url }}) has transmitted footage of the battle. As the party recovers from their injuries, they gain a new ally in Jet, an avian ranger seeking to avenge his fallen comrade. With weeks of preparation complete, the council deploys a massive force to establish a forward operating base at the former LB&S campsite—just in time to witness WoodCo's assembled army marching up the mountain, their bulldozers glowing with stolen Scarlet Wood magic.

## Overview

The party presents evidence to the council, analyzes the WoodCo agent's cell phone with [Rook Gearclaw]({{ "/world/npcs/rook-gearclaw/" | relative_url }}) and [Mistgleam]({{ "/world/npcs/mistgleam/" | relative_url }}), and joins a massive deployment to establish defensive positions. As construction completes on the forward base, WoodCo mobilizes a heavily-armed force equipped with weapons, vehicles, and magic-imbued bulldozers built from the Scarlet Wood itself.

## Details

### Return and Council Meeting

- The party arrives at the Scarlet Wood carrying Basil's body and evidence from the [Lumberjack Billy & Sons]({{ "/world/organizations/lumberjack-billy-and-sons/" | relative_url }}) crew
- The council holds an emergency meeting to hear the party's report
- The council applauds the party's victory while mourning the fallen ranger Basil
- [Rook Gearclaw]({{ "/world/npcs/rook-gearclaw/" | relative_url }}) expresses particular interest in the "black rectangle" (cell phone) recovered from the WoodCo agent
- The council orders the party to rest and recover while [Orven Taloncrest]({{ "/world/npcs/orven-taloncrest/" | relative_url }}) mobilizes heightened scouting patrols throughout the Wood

### Recovery and New Ally

- During recovery, the Scarlet Wood bustles with activity as young ranger squads deploy on scouting missions
- Tales of the party's battle and Basil's sacrifice spread throughout the Wood
- Jet, an avian ranger and member of the [Sky-Sailors]({{ "/world/factions/sky-sailors/" | relative_url }}) faction, approaches the party
- Jet offers his condolences for Basil's death and requests to join the group to avenge his fallen comrade
- The party welcomes Jet as their newest member

### Analyzing WoodCo Technology

- The party meets with [Rook Gearclaw]({{ "/world/npcs/rook-gearclaw/" | relative_url }}) at the [Scrapsmiths]({{ "/world/factions/scrapsmiths/" | relative_url }}) home base within the great hollow tree
- Climbing to Rook's laboratory at the tree's apex, they find [Mistgleam]({{ "/world/npcs/mistgleam/" | relative_url }}) already present
- The assembled group analyzes the cell phone and reaches two critical conclusions:
  1. The device functions as a viewfinder or telescope, evidenced by its lens and the WoodCo agent's usage pattern
  2. The text "Upload Complete" indicates that images captured through the device have been transmitted to an external location—most likely a [WoodCo]({{ "/world/organizations/woodco/" | relative_url }}) management facility or headquarters
- The party reconvenes with the council to share this alarming discovery
- The council begins strategic planning for an anticipated [WoodCo]({{ "/world/organizations/woodco/" | relative_url }}) response

### Preparation and Deployment

- Over the following weeks, the party trains and prepares for combat
- [Foreman Bristleback]({{ "/world/npcs/foreman-bristleback/" | relative_url }}) leads a planning group to design defensive fortifications
- The council assembles all available forces and issues deployment orders:
  - [Foreman Bristleback]({{ "/world/npcs/foreman-bristleback/" | relative_url }}) will lead the expedition
  - Nearly one hundred [Earth-Movers]({{ "/world/factions/earth-movers/" | relative_url }}) and [Scrapsmiths]({{ "/world/factions/scrapsmiths/" | relative_url }}) will construct fortifications
  - Nearly fifty rangers will provide defense and scouting
  - The objective: claim the former [Lumberjack Billy & Sons]({{ "/world/organizations/lumberjack-billy-and-sons/" | relative_url }}) campsite and establish a forward operating base to defend against [WoodCo]({{ "/world/organizations/woodco/" | relative_url }}) advance towards The Scarlet Wood

### Forward Base Construction

- The party travels with the massive deployment force toward the LB&S campsite
- After several days of slow travel, they arrive to find the site unchanged except for the burnt-out trailer
- [Earth-Movers]({{ "/world/factions/earth-movers/" | relative_url }}) and [Scrapsmiths]({{ "/world/factions/scrapsmiths/" | relative_url }}) work together to harvest nearby timber, stone, and debris
- An earthen barricade takes shape over the coming days
- Regular scouting reports reveal that [WoodCo]({{ "/world/organizations/woodco/" | relative_url }}) is assembling their own forward operating base at the mountain's edge near the main road

### WoodCo Mobilizes

- After the barricade's completion, scouts report that [WoodCo]({{ "/world/organizations/woodco/" | relative_url }}) is mobilizing their forces
- Multiple trucks loaded with heavily-armed agents wielding guns and chainsaws begin ascending the mountain
- Three massive bulldozer-like machines follow behind the truck convoy
- Non-combatants retreat behind the barricade as ranger squads take defensive positions
- Six loaded trucks peel off to flank the position while three bulldozers advance down the center
- The party observes the WoodCo forces' superior equipment: body armor, long guns, sidearms, and large chainsaws
- Examining the bulldozers more closely, the party makes a horrifying discovery:
  - The machines are only partially constructed from metal
  - Much of their internal workings consist of high-quality wood
  - The machines glow faintly from within with a deep, scarlet light
  - [WoodCo]({{ "/world/organizations/woodco/" | relative_url }}) has imbued these bulldozers with the Scarlet Wood's own stolen magic
