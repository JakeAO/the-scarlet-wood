The battle at the ambush site erupted into violence as the PCs stormed the [Lumberjack Billy & Sons]({{ "/world/organizations/lumberjack-billy-and-sons/" | relative_url }}) crew. Despite the WoodCo agent's attempt to document the encounter, the PCs emerged victorious—but at a heavy cost. Basil the lizard fell to the tracking dogs, marking the party's first casualty. With [Billy Sr.]({{ "/world/enemies/billy-sr/" | relative_url }}) and his sons defeated, the PCs harvested resources from their fallen enemies and returned to the Scarlet Wood carrying evidence of WoodCo's operations and the body of their fallen companion.

## Overview

The PCs engaged in fierce combat with the Lumberjack Billy & Sons crew, defeating Billy Sr., Billy Jr., Tommy Billy, and their tracking dogs. The battle claimed the life of Basil, but the party secured critical evidence and eliminated a major threat to the Scarlet Wood.

## Key Characters

- **[Billy Sr.]({{ "/world/enemies/billy-sr/" | relative_url }})** - Patriarch of Lumberjack Billy & Sons, killed by Stenbjorn
- **[Billy Jr.]({{ "/world/enemies/billy-jr/" | relative_url }})** - Billy's son, killed by Howard
- **Tommy Billy** - Billy's son, killed by Stenbjorn
- **WoodCo Agent** - Corporate representative who recorded footage of the battle before being incapacitated

## Details

- The [Lumberjack Billy & Sons]({{ "/world/organizations/lumberjack-billy-and-sons/" | relative_url }}) crew emerged from their two trucks: [Billy Sr.]({{ "/world/enemies/billy-sr/" | relative_url }}), [Billy Jr.]({{ "/world/enemies/billy-jr/" | relative_url }}), Tommy Billy, four tracking dogs, and the [WoodCo]({{ "/world/organizations/woodco/" | relative_url }}) agent
- The PCs immediately launched a brutal assault on the crew
- The WoodCo agent fled up the gully's side and began recording the battle with his cell phone—a device the PCs did not recognize
- Howard killed [Billy Jr.]({{ "/world/enemies/billy-jr/" | relative_url }}) with a slashing attack
- Stenbjorn killed Tommy Billy with a crushing hammer blow
- One of the LB&S tracking dogs fatally wounded Basil in a savage bite
- The PCs defeated the remaining tracking dogs
- Stenbjorn finally brought down [Billy Sr.]({{ "/world/enemies/billy-sr/" | relative_url }})
- With the WoodCo agent as the only combatant remaining, the party charged after him
- The WoodCo agent successfully completed uploading the recorded footage before the PCs reached him
- Thaddius felled the WoodCo agent with a well-placed slingshot stone
- The PCs harvested materials and components from their defeated enemies for future crafting
- The party confiscated the WoodCo agent's cell phone as evidence
- The PCs returned to the Scarlet Wood carrying Basil's body and the evidence gathered from [Lumberjack Billy & Sons]({{ "/world/organizations/lumberjack-billy-and-sons/" | relative_url }}) and [WoodCo]({{ "/world/organizations/woodco/" | relative_url }})
