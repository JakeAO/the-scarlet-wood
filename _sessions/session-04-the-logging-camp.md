Following vehicle tracks and damaged forest areas, the PCs located a [Lumberjack Billy & Sons]({{ "/world/organizations/lumberjack-billy-and-sons/" | relative_url }}) logging camp outside the Scarlet Wood. They infiltrated the site, searched a trailer office, and discovered documents revealing [WoodCo]({{ "/world/organizations/woodco/" | relative_url }})'s involvement in coordinated logging operations. The sample taken from the Scarlet Wood was specially marked as significant. After stealing key documents, the PCs set the trailer ablaze and escaped.

## Overview

The PCs tracked logging operations to a remote camp, infiltrated the site while only one logger was present, and stole documents revealing WoodCo's search for "resonant samples." They destroyed the trailer office before retreating.

## Key Characters

- **[Billy Jr.]({{ "/world/enemies/billy-jr/" | relative_url }})** - A logger left alone at the camp during the PCs' infiltration

## Details

- The PCs tracked logging activity by following damaged forest areas and vehicle tracks
- They discovered multiple small clear-cut sites, each less than half an acre in size
- The PCs located a logging camp consisting of pickup trucks, stacked logs, and a single-wide trailer used as an office
- Only one logger was present at the camp during the infiltration
- The PCs infiltrated the site, scavenged supplies, and searched the trailer
- Inside the trailer, they found paperwork marked with the LB&S ([Lumberjack Billy & Sons]({{ "/world/organizations/lumberjack-billy-and-sons/" | relative_url }})) logo
- Documents included [WoodCo]({{ "/world/organizations/woodco/" | relative_url }}) letters, bills of sale, work orders, and a regional map marking logged sites and sample locations
- The sample taken from the Scarlet Wood was specially marked and treated as particularly significant
- The PCs stole key documents and set the trailer on fire before escaping
