Ignoring [Orven]({{ "/world/npcs/orven-taloncrest/" | relative_url }})'s explicit orders and warnings, the PCs approached a human camp northeast of the Scarlet Wood. They engaged three hunters and their dogs in combat, achieving victory and discovering [WoodCo]({{ "/world/organizations/woodco/" | relative_url }})-branded products at the campsite. Though the hunters weren't directly responsible for the logging, this encounter marked the first evidence linking the corporate entity to activity near the Scarlet Wood.

## Overview

The PCs ignored orders to avoid the human hunters, engaged them in combat, and discovered WoodCo-branded equipment at their camp. Investigation revealed ongoing logging activity spanning approximately one month.

## Details

- The PCs approached the human camp despite [Orven]({{ "/world/npcs/orven-taloncrest/" | relative_url }})'s orders to avoid contact
- Combat ensued with three human hunters and their dogs; the PCs were victorious
- Investigation of the camp revealed WoodCo-branded products, though the hunters were not directly responsible for the logging operations
- The PCs returned to the Forest Guard rendezvous point
- Reports from all trainee groups confirmed that logging had occurred over approximately one month using small-scale tools and vehicles
- The trainees were ordered back to the core of the Scarlet Wood to attend a council meeting convened to address the incursion
